Interactive Circuit Simulator (Live Browser Edition)

Interactive Dual-Pipeline Visualization of Observer Mechanics vs. Standard QM

This interactive application operationalizes the Universal Principle of Collapse (UPC) directly in your browser, providing a step-by-step comparative trace between observer-indexed mechanics and standard quantum frameworks. Built as an active digital extension of our previously published study, The UPC Visual Simulator Demonstrating Observer Mechanics, this live edition allows you to input custom semantic strings, step through transformation operators from Potential State (|PO>) to Classical Trace (T), and audit how semantic fields map to eigenstate proximity in real time.

Metrological Notice: This live browser edition utilizes a lightweight TF-IDF vectorizer (MO) for WebAssembly compatibility. As a result, it trades deep semantic resolution for instant execution, occasionally producing boundary projection artifacts on charged text. Deeper text evaluations and full-context analysis are supported by the local Python SDK, which draws on a richer semantic space.
